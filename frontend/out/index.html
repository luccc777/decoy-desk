How it decides

Red

A hard rule fired (a seized domain, a program named like a PDF, a hidden PowerShell command, a "fix your camera" command), or the score reached 50.

Yellow

The score is 20 to 49, or we could check less than 60% of what applies. A check that failed never counts as safe.

Green

Nothing above. It isn't a guarantee.

The color comes from a published rules table. A language model reads the conversation and writes the explanation, but it never picks the color, and anything it claims must quote the conversation word for word.
